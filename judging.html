Weighted Rubric

How Projects Are Evaluated

Every submission is reviewed blindly by at least three independent evaluators across 5 core criteria.

25% Weight

Innovation & Novelty

Degree of technological or business model originality. Does the solution offer a uniquely African, leapfrog approach?

25% Weight

Agenda 2063 Impact

Measurable socio-economic or environmental potential aligned with specific AU Agenda 2063 flagship projects and SDGs.

20% Weight

Technical Feasibility

Soundness of engineering, architecture, prototype demo, and technological viability under real-world African infrastructure constraints.

15% Weight

Market Scalability

Clear path to unit economics, AfCFTA regulatory compliance, and potential to expand beyond the home country into pan-African markets.

15% Weight

Team & Execution

Complementary multidisciplinary skillsets, gender diversity, and clarity demonstrated during live pitches and Q&A.

Transparency Pledge

Academic Integrity & IP Ownership

AUC is dedicated to protecting undergraduate student intellectual property. All team IP remains 100% owned by the student inventors. All evaluators and mentors sign strict NDAs prior to reviewing submissions.

Automated Verification Protocols

  • Double-blind peer reviews in preliminary stages
  • Automated code and literature originality verification
  • Live technical code walkthroughs for all 15 finalists
  • Independent audit committee overseen by AU Advisory Board
