Competition Architecture

Four Stages to the Continental Crown

A rigorous, merit-driven trajectory providing mentorship, technical validation, and seed funding at each phase.

Stage 01 · Campus Level

Intra-University Ideation Sprints

Multi-disciplinary student teams form on local university campuses to submit proof-of-concept solutions aligned to community challenges.

Stage 02 · National Level

National Championships

Top 5% of university teams pitch before national juries, government ministries, and local venture hubs.

Stage 03 · Regional Hubs

5 Regional Innovation Semi-Finals

Semi-finalists in North, West, East, Central, and Southern Africa undergo a 4-week virtual accelerator with industry mentors.

Stage 04 · Continental Finals

Grand Continental Demo Day & Gala

Top 15 continental finalist teams pitch live at the AU Headquarters for seed grants, venture investment, and continental awards.
